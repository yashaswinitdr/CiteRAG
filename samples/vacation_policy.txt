Acme Robotics — Employee Vacation Policy

All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
Vacation accrual begins on the employee's start date and is prorated for
partial years. Unused vacation days may be carried over into the following
year, up to a maximum carryover of 5 days.

Employees must submit vacation requests through the HR portal at least 10
business days in advance for any leave longer than 3 consecutive days.
Requests shorter than 3 days require only 2 business days notice.

Vacation requests during the last two weeks of December require manager
approval at least 30 days in advance due to holiday staffing constraints.

Part-time employees accrue vacation on a prorated basis according to their
scheduled weekly hours, calculated as (weekly hours / 40) * 18 days.
