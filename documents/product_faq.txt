Acme Robotics — TidyBot 3000 Product FAQ

Q: How long does the TidyBot 3000's battery last on a single charge?
A: The TidyBot 3000 runs for approximately 90 minutes of continuous cleaning
on a full charge, and takes 3 hours to fully recharge from empty.

Q: Is the TidyBot 3000 safe to use around pets?
A: Yes. The TidyBot 3000 uses infrared proximity sensors to detect pets and
will automatically stop and reroute around them. It also emits a low-frequency
alert tone that most cats and dogs will simply ignore.

Q: What is the warranty period?
A: The TidyBot 3000 ships with a 2-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. Battery degradation below 70% capacity within the
first 12 months is also covered.

Q: Can the TidyBot 3000 clean stairs?
A: No. The TidyBot 3000 is designed for single-level flooring only and has
cliff-detection sensors that will stop it at the edge of a staircase.
